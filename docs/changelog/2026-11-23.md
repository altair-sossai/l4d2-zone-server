## Team lines of the community ranking mix

- At the end of **`!rmix`**, the names of each team are separated by a white `|` and shown in the color of the team: *"Survivors: a | b | c | d"*.
- Long names are cut after 12 characters (9 when the teams have 5 or more players), without `..` at the end. Accents and symbols count as one character, so those names are no longer cut much shorter than the others.
- The list of players who are not in the community ranking yet uses the same `|`.
- [`l4d2_mix`](../plugins/README.md#l4d2_mix-adapted) is now version 5.1.1.
