# Server changelog — 4 August 2026

Summary of the changes released between 28 July 2026 and 4 August 2026, including new features and adjustments affecting players.

## Name plugin

- **Fixed player names:** the plugin now maintains a list of names that selected players must use, preventing frequent changes and making everybody easier to identify.
- File: [standardised-name list](../cfg/sourcemod/forced_names.cfg).

## Queue and teams

- **Persistence:** queue order is preserved after quick restarts or reloads and can be recovered for up to **30 minutes**.
- **Reconnection:** disconnected players keep their position for **5 minutes** so they can return.
- **Display:** the queue appears after the end-of-map statistics, and active players are highlighted in **blue**.
- **Priority:** slots go to the first connected players in the queue, with no swaps when teams are already correct.
- **Scoring:** the reorganisation calculation was corrected so duplicated scores cannot affect positions.
- Files: [l4d2_queue.sp](../addons/sourcemod/scripting/l4d2_queue.sp) and [queue messages](../addons/sourcemod/translations/l4d2_queue.phrases.txt).

## `!vaga` and `!slot`

- **New commands:** `!vaga` and `!slot` let a spectator claim a slot according to their queue position.
- **Availability:** only during ready-up in a **0–0 match**, with **full teams** and **no mix** in progress.
- **Queue order:** the requester can only replace an active player behind them and cannot skip ahead.
- **Slot swap:** the lower-priority player moves to spectators and the requester takes their team; both receive an explanation.
- **Notices:** eligible spectators may be notified automatically, while attempts during team organisation must wait.
- Files: [command logic](../addons/sourcemod/scripting/l4d2_queue.sp) and [command messages](../addons/sourcemod/translations/l4d2_queue.phrases.txt).

## Votes

- **Kick votes:** player-initiated votes have been **disabled**.
- Vote configuration: [server.cfg](../cfg/server.cfg).

## Early victory

- **Detection:** during the second round of the **fourth map**, the server identifies when the result is mathematically decided.
- **Vote:** the losing team has **15 seconds** to vote; the match continues if at least **half** of the eligible players choose to continue.
- **Conclusion:** if they stop, everybody is eliminated after **5 seconds** and a new campaign starts **5 seconds** later.
- **Next campaign:** randomly selected from **10 official campaigns** — Dead Center, Dark Carnival, Swamp Fever, Hard Rain, The Parish, No Mercy, Death Toll, Dead Air, Blood Harvest and Cold Stream.
- **Exceptions:** inactive during ready-up, finales and matches without human players on the losing team.
- **Map change blocked:** on the **fourth map**, during the **second round**, players cannot start a manual campaign-change vote; they must wait for the round to turn or concede it.
- Files: [l4d2_early_victory.sp](../addons/sourcemod/scripting/l4d2_early_victory.sp), [early-victory messages](../addons/sourcemod/translations/l4d2_early_victory.phrases.txt) and [configuration](../cfg/cfgogl/zonemod/shared_cvars.cfg).

## Mix

- **Votes:** the first mix requires **2 votes**; further attempts before live require **2 additional votes** each, up to the player limit.
- **Reset:** the requirement returns to its initial value when the map changes or the round begins.
- **Restrictions:** requires **full teams**, is unavailable in **1v1**, and may be started directly by authorised administrators.
- **Cancellation:** votes and pending selections are cleared so one attempt cannot affect the next.
- **Player departure:** the mix is cancelled if a participant disconnects during selection.
- Files: [l4d2_mix.sp](../addons/sourcemod/scripting/l4d2_mix.sp), [mix messages](../addons/sourcemod/translations/l4d2_mix.phrases.txt) and [configuration](../cfg/cfgogl/zonemod/shared_cvars.cfg).

## Related accounts

- **Detection:** a notice may appear when other accounts are related to a player's connection history.
- **Details:** chat shows a summary, while related names, Steam identifiers and profiles are available in the console.
- **No automatic punishment:** the feature is for identification only and does not issue bans by itself.
- **Visibility:** notices are highlighted in **green**.
- Files: [l4d2_gameinfo_sync.sp](../addons/sourcemod/scripting/l4d2_gameinfo_sync.sp) and [related-account messages](../addons/sourcemod/translations/l4d2_gameinfo_sync.phrases.txt).

## Spectators and AFK

- **When it applies:** AFK players may be moved during ready-up when teams are full and somebody is waiting.
- **Activity:** movement, in-game actions and chat messages refresh the player's timer.
- **Limits:** **3 minutes** at the start of a match and **6 minutes** during later ready-ups.
- **Notice:** the server announces who was moved and their inactive time.
- **Live rounds:** the system is disabled, so nobody is removed for being AFK while playing.
- Files: [l4d2_afk_to_spec.sp](../addons/sourcemod/scripting/l4d2_afk_to_spec.sp), [AFK messages](../addons/sourcemod/translations/l4d2_afk_to_spec.phrases.txt) and [l4d2_admin_spec_lock.sp](../addons/sourcemod/scripting/l4d2_admin_spec_lock.sp).
